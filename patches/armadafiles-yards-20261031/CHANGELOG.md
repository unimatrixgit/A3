# ArmadaFiles yard replacements - 31 October 2026

55 production-yard entries across every faction now use complete downloaded hulls. Production, costs, research and weapons are retained. Visual scales are normalized and GUI icons match the models. Dedicated repair docks remain unchanged. No station hulls were merged.

Some tiers share a source model where the catalogue lacks a suitable separate yard. Bajor uses Cardassian industrial yards.
