# Mining Yards and miner refits

Nine new native Mining Yards: Federation, Borg, Klingon, Romulan, Cardassian, Dominion, Bajoran, Breen and Species 8472. Each offers its Basic Collector (MK I), MK II and MK III. Build from the basic constructor; Species 8472 also has the option on its construction rigs. Models use the existing native SOD graph, faction textures and separate production/repair anchors.

Ferengi miners remain in Ferengi Service Yards. Both the Latinum-priced and Borg-priced Service Yards gain a starbase-like appearance while retaining their existing production, constructors, prices and durability. Dedicated dilithium and latinum collectors remain available. Combined-resource Ferengi refits require both matching Dilithium and Latinum Contracts; Borg variants require Dilithium Contracts only and do not gain Latinum equipment.

Research unlocks new-construction tier choices AND a single forward Upgrade button on existing miners. The button uses the game's one-unit NX fusingweapon pattern: MK I to MK II, then MK III. It costs no special energy. Unload cargo before refitting. Cargo transfer, health/crew transfer and automatic mining resumption require in-game validation; no automatic-refit behavior is claimed. New production does not automatically pick or hide tiers.

Native dilithium equipment receives 50% / 100% increased cargo capacity and extraction rate, relative to its own basic unit. Native latinum/metal beams remain unchanged. Species 8472 uses its existing Biomatter Extraction I/II abilities, with matching 50% / 100% biomatter cargo capacity. Federation contracts use dilithium/metal and occupy two separate physical sockets at the advanced research station. Other conventional factions use existing Ferengi Dilithium Contracts; Borg retains its no-Latinum contracts.

Display naming: Dilithium Collector MK I–III, Assemblers MK I–III, Orbital Mining Station MK I–III for existing tiers. Latinum and biomatter collectors keep resource-appropriate names. No ODF filenames change. Existing orbital gameplay is unchanged: planet-attached station refits are deliberately not included pending a safe attachment test.

All 45 faction AI build plans include the native yard before native miner orders and researched tiers after prerequisite research. The existing opening economy and starting fleets are preserved. Build icons have resource/tier indicators, yards retain yard/repair badges, and full RGB queue/status portraits are preserved.

Static validation is recorded separately from gameplay validation. Test a new Standard Technology match, build the yard, research the contracts, unload a collector and use its Upgrade button. Verify ownership, cargo, crew, mining, launch position, repair and production portraits. Full Tech intentionally bypasses research requirements.
