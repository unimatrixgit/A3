# Upgraded resource, science and Borg utility models

Concept images were generated with the built-in image generation tool; prompts are saved in prompts.json. Game models are native geometry assemblies with faction materials and matching model-based icons.

Applied to D:/armadaIII after explicit user approval and confirmation that the game was closed. The pack covers 67 models: 18 freighters, 11 refineries, all 34 research facilities (including the Technology Assimilator), two additional assimilation structures, the Borg Recycler and Borg Repair Drone.

All 184 research pod mounts are supplied and visibly supported by the station geometry. Upgrade centers, both Omega laboratories, Sela's Research, the Ferengi institutes and Species 8472 facilities are included. Existing primary and secondary research menus and effects are retained. Species 8472 keeps biomatter functionality.

The assimilation structures use a shared Borg core. The Assimilation Station retains xbrepair production and has a visible open bay with a corresponding build anchor. Existing green and blue assimilation weapon definitions remain unchanged. No Borg cube loadouts are altered.

Other orbital miners grow by 20 percent. The active Borg orbital and its legacy counterpart match the enlarged Federation orbital's world-space model span (76.8 units). Placement buffers are scaled proportionally; resources, crew, weapons and mining statistics are unchanged.

One pre-existing malformed Federation upgrade-center hardpoint assignment was normalized to its intended quoted hardpoint list. All other existing ODF fields remain unchanged apart from model references and the requested orbital scales / footprints.

Validation passed 9,730 static checks, and all 298 installed hashes matched the manifest. This does not establish gameplay success. Please test mining, unloading, pod construction at both research tiers, Omega research, assimilation and repair-drone production in a new match.
