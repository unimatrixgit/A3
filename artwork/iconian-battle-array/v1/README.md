# Iconian Battle Arrays

Concept artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool; prompt saved in prompt.txt. The shared faction material source is saved alongside the concept sheet. Actual game geometry and texture mapping are generated by scripts/stage-iconian-battle-arrays.py.

Nine faction variants are named Iconian Battle Array. The existing Borg Tactical Battle Array is redesigned separately and keeps its assimilation beam and transwarp. The other arrays have neither Borg ability.

| Faction | Constructor | Extra abilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federation | Advanced Constructor | Corbomite Reflector |
| Klingon | Advanced Constructor | Ion Cannon |
| Romulan | Advanced Constructor | Cloak |
| Cardassian | Advanced Constructor | Plasma Bank Overload and Cloak |
| Dominion | Advanced Constructor | Jem'Hadar Warriors |
| Bajoran | Advanced Constructor | No extra ability requested |
| Species 8472 | Advanced Constructor | No extra ability requested |
| Breen | Single constructor | No extra ability requested |
| Ferengi | Single constructor | No extra ability requested |
| Borg | Existing Advanced Constructor entry | Assimilation and Transwarp retained |

All arrays preserve the source array's six beam banks, four torpedo banks, 22,000 shield health, 1,460 shield regeneration, 200,000 special energy, 20,000 energy recharge, system health and construction time. Conventional costs remain 9,500 dilithium, 16,500 metal and 5,000 crew; maximum crew is 5,000. Species 8472 instead pays 26,000 biomatter and no crew cost to suit its economy.

Native research gates apply to Corbomite Reflector, Ion Cannon, Plasma Bank Overload and Jem'Hadar boarding. Cloak is available without additional research. Full Tech and No Special Weapons modes have their corresponding rules.

Arrays produce their faction's builders and resource freighters. Species 8472 produces embryos and constructors and handles biomatter rather than dilithium. All variants have matching build icons, full-image status portraits and valid production and docking hardpoints. No starting fleets changed.

The static validation passed 1,019 checks. This is not a gameplay test. In-game construction, freighter unloading, cloak, research gating, ability use and match results still require testing.
