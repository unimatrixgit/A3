# Yard consolidation

Applied to D:/armadaIII. Runtime testing pending.

Retired 19 redundant yards. Preserved all 340 units from yard menus. All 45 AI plans pass sequential prerequisite/producer checks; all formerly reachable units remain reachable.

Menus have at most 14 unique entries. Spare slots preserve sensible early/advanced/specialist production roles; no duplicate ships were invented to fill slots. Dedicated repair, recycling, assimilation, superweapon and wormhole facilities retain their separate functions. The already-unavailable fbyard remains unavailable.

Native hull/shield statistics, crew, repair settings, models, starting fleets, ship abilities and existing artwork are preserved. Conventional basic yards cost 250 dilithium / 750 metal and take 45 seconds; advanced yards 300 / 1000 and 90 seconds; elite yards 450 / 1350 and 120 seconds. Other resource charges and specialized facilities retain their existing prices.
