# Cost balance

All named playable-race ships and stations use the same capability formula. Military ships: 100 + shields*0.35 + estimated DPS*3 + shield regeneration*15 + special abilities*60 + crew*0.08. Utility ships start at 150 and builders add 120. Stations: 400 + shields*0.14 + estimated DPS*0.85 + production*350 + specials*45. Rounded to 50 resources. Non-organic units split cost 55% dilithium and 45% metal; Species 8472 pays the equivalent total in biomatter. Latinum costs are zero for equal access across factions; trade and display remain available. Existing research cost totals are retained and normalized; Omega research costs 4000. Build times scale with total cost.

This is a consistent economic baseline, not a claim of proven competitive balance. Original combat stats are preserved except explicit hero/Omega variants. The balance tables include campaign heroes and faction utility units.
