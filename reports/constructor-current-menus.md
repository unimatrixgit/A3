# Current constructor names and menus

Applied to D:/armadaIII. Names and tooltips changed; icons remain unchanged. Borg constructors retain Transwarp Drive,1000 special energy and10/sec recharge.

Breen have one native Basic Constructor. Ferengi are a mercenary subrace with one Temporal Constructor per purchasing catalog; the Borg catalog uses dilithium/metal prices. Other factions retain Basic/Advanced/Temporal roles. Foreign starbases remain available to Breen through Ferengi contracts.
