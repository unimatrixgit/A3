*****************CARADASSIAN DETAPA CLASS***************
*****************README FILE*************************
*****************Version 1**************************

When the Federation and Klingons ended their occupation of the Cardassian Union. Central Command ordered 2 new battleship prototypes. The winner of the contest was the mighty Hutet. The Detapa being a capable ship in it's own right was refit into a fleet command battleship. They are armed with several multi-targeting phasers 'borrowed' from the Federation and a more powerful plasma bank, and command and control systems that increase the combat abilities of nearby allied ships.
